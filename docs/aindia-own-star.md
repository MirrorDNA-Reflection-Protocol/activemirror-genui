# AIndia Own Star

Date: 2026-06-12

## Provenance

This is doctrine derived only from user-provided direction in this build thread.

It is not an external factual claim. It is not a biography. It is not "Paul thinks" language. It is a product compass built from explicit statements:

- help, not extraction
- trust by design
- reflection over prediction
- latency and cheap devices are priority
- do not make anything up
- people are lost and need the right philosophy
- founder relay only with consent
- India-specific without cringe

## The Star

```text
Active Mirror is Sovereign AI for India.

AIndia exists to help people make safer, clearer decisions in their language,
on the devices they actually have,
without extracting trust from them.
```

Boundary:

This is user-provided product positioning. It means Active Mirror owns the trust harness: local-first routes, consent, source-bound claims, deterministic gates, receipts, replay, and human relay.

It does not claim:

- government endorsement
- monopoly
- perfect model certainty
- unsupported factual superiority

## Axioms

1. Help, not extraction.
2. Trust by design.
3. Reflection over prediction.
4. Latency and cheap devices first.
5. No made-up facts.
6. Human relay with consent.
7. India without performance.

## Decision Rules

| Pressure | Choose |
|---|---|
| Accuracy vs speed | Give a safe fast next step, then offer sourced depth. |
| Power vs trust | Keep the local/consented route even if a cloud model would be flashier. |
| Growth vs dignity | Do not use confusion, fear, or low literacy as conversion pressure. |
| Model capability vs deterministic behavior | Let the model propose, but keep the harness as authority. |
| Future vision vs factual rigor | Label scenarios as scenarios and ship only verified runtime claims. |

## Anti-Star

Reject:

- frontier-model ego
- chatbot costume
- confusion as capture
- fake certainty
- flagship-only AI

## Runtime Meaning

This star must compile into gates:

- claim guard
- consent envelope
- tiny route
- source packs
- receipt writer
- replay verifier
- relay consent
- low-end-device budget

If it cannot compile into a gate, it is only a slogan.
