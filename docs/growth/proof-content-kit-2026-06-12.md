## Founder Post 1

Most AI demos are theatre.

They show a clean prompt, a clean answer, and hide the parts that matter at work:

- What source was used?
- What was assumed?
- What is missing?
- What needs approval?
- What can safely happen next?

Active Mirror is built for the messy part.

Send us one workflow your current AI cannot safely finish. If it fits, we return a working governed proof in 72 hours. If it does not fit, you get a clear no.

## Founder Post 3

AI work fails in the handoff.

Active Mirror turns the request into reviewable work:

- a deploy-or-don't recommendation
